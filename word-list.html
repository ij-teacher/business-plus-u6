READING / VOCABULARY

Words to Know

Explore 14 words from the hotel reviews. Read each definition and sample sentence, then return to the reading.

Words, definitions, and sample sentences
WordDefinitionSample sentence
ReviewA written opinion saying if something is good or badBefore booking a hotel room, I always read online customer reviews.
IdealPerfect or the best possible choiceSunny weather with a light breeze is ideal for a picnic in the park.
UnfortunatelyUnluckily; used to share bad or sad newsI wanted to join you for dinner, but unfortunately, I have to work late tonight.
SignalA sign, sound, or action that tells someone to do somethingShe raised her hand as a signal for the driver to stop.
DisadvantageSomething that causes a problem or makes things harderOne disadvantage of living far away is the long travel time.
FantasticVery good or greatThe chocolate cake at the bakery tasted fantastic.
StaffThe people who work for a business or companyThe hotel staff was friendly and helped us with our luggage.
FacilityA building, room, or equipment built for a specific purposeThe university campus offers excellent athletic facilities, including a pool and a gym.
ConferenceA big meeting where people gather to talk about a topicDoctors from around the world gathered for the annual medical conference.
ComfortableMaking you feel relaxed and physically at easeThis soft armchair is very comfortable to sit in while reading.
CrowdedFull of too many peopleThe subway station gets extremely crowded during peak commute hours.
RecommendTo suggest something because you think it is goodCan you recommend a good Italian restaurant nearby?
LocationA specific place or positionThe new coffee shop is in a convenient location right next to the train station.
UnattractiveNot nice or pretty to look atThe old, empty building looked very unattractive.
